# Doctrine priorities before NIMOI launch
2026-09-29 · recommendation, not doctrine

## What I counted as doctrine, and how I ranked it

Doctrine here means a cross-session commitment that governs authority, permissible change, evidence, or institutional memory. A map, a tool, a procedure for one task, and a successful precedent are inputs, not standing doctrine merely because they exist. Founder language becomes standing only by explicit designation or confirmation; the current onboarding already makes that distinction. [checked: read origins/onboarding_1.12.md, Authority section] The older origins/NIMOI.md identifies constitutional *candidates* and says a constitution is owed; I have not promoted those candidates by inference. [checked: read origins/NIMOI.md, Lineage and Present state; nimoi_mapping_project/efforts/E01_constitution_onboarding_virtues.md §5]

My ordering rule is: (1) irreversible loss or falsification of the failure record, unauthorized authority growth, or involuntary disclosure; (2) how directly an unresolved rule would affect the first governed self-modification; (3) how much can be settled with a short founder decision rather than a new system. This follows NIMOI's stated bar—real attempts whose failures remain informative, with record-corrupting defects ranked above ordinary failures—not a shipping checklist. [checked: read origins/persistent_testbed.md §§2–6] The map's large counts and defaults are leads, not votes. [checked: read nimoi_mapping_project/MAP.md opening caveat; FOUNDER_QUESTIONS.md opening]

I recommend a **thin, ratified launch kernel**, then one real governed change under it, then revision from what breaks. This is a proposed sequence, not a declaration that NIMOI has already adopted these rules. Do not hold launch for a comprehensive constitution, a complete legacy-failure harvest, a unified procedure library, or a finished reorganization. [working: prioritization judgment based on sources below]

## Ranked decisions

### P0 / launch gate 1 — Authority and amendment envelope

**Founder decision:** Ratify one short charter for the launch window: which artifacts agents may propose and change, which changes require independent review or founder authorization, who can stop a run, how a worker objects to malformed work, and how the charter itself may be amended. Distinguish a standing directive from a task instruction at the point it is recorded; retain the dated source and an explicit status rather than making every chat utterance law. Put non-delegable powers (for example widening permissions or public release) outside the initial agent grant unless the founder expressly decides otherwise. [working: proposed rule]

**Why first:** Full governed self-modification begins at launch, but the historical constitution is unwritten; the map says doctrine froze while three practice regimes arose beside it. [checked: read origins/onboarding_1.12.md, Current stage; nimoi_mapping_project/efforts/E01_constitution_onboarding_virtues.md §5; relationships/themes.md T10] Onboarding now grants escalation and justified dissent without granting concealment or unilateral scope expansion. [checked: read origins/onboarding_1.12.md, Organizational responsibility] That is a sound seed, not a complete change jurisdiction. Existing harness bounds are enforcement mechanisms; doctrine must state the *authority* they implement. [working]

**Minimum artifact / pass condition:** A dated v0.1 charter with owner, effective scope, protected surfaces, grant matrix, amendment path, and a pointer from the entry contract. A fresh agent should be able to classify a proposed change as allowed, approval-needed, or out of scope without guessing from a map default. [working]

### P0 / launch gate 2 — Failure intake and record integrity

**Founder decision:** Choose the institutional intake route and whether run deviations and harness-side memory count as NIMOI memory (map FQ-37/FQ-38). Permit local detailed formats, but require a durable cross-lane pointer for each material failure or discrepancy: stable identifier, source run/artifact, expected and observed, evidence/claim status, impact on record integrity, open/resolved state, and next owner or explicit closure. Route transferable lessons to findings as a separate promotion step. [working: proposed minimum, not an adopted schema]

**Why now:** Two failure-record conventions do not refer to one another; the directed failure-record procedure has an entry point, while the map reports one use. [checked: read procedures/failure_record.md and nimoi_mapping_project/efforts/E02_failure_records.md §§1–3] The map reports hundreds of numbered deviations in run records and further campaign/harness stores, versus four filed failure records and nine general findings; I did not recount those objects. [working: map E02 §§1–2 and E03 §5, historical through 2026-09-20] The finding procedure has a useful admission test but is a different product from a failure record. [checked: read procedures/general_findings.md and procedures/failure_record.md] Treating the four filed records as the total institutional failure history would make the record misleading. [working: inference from map E02 §7f]

**Minimum artifact / pass condition:** One accessible index connecting a new launch-window failure to its raw run record, investigation, correction, and any general finding; a cold reader can tell what was not checked. Keep original records addressable and label supersession. Do **not** retroactively classify the historical deviation pile before launch; start a bounded harvest after the standard survives actual use. [working]

### P0 / launch gate 3 — Governed change with evidence and independent challenge

**Founder decision:** Define risk tiers for self-modification. A proposal should state its bar and expected failure mode; preserve a before state; name who made, checked, and accepted the change; attach receipts; and say how to reverse or supersede it. Record an adverse or inconclusive result rather than quietly rewriting the claim. Record-affecting changes need stronger independent checks than ordinary reversible work. Let ordinary failures occur under observation: zero failures is not the objective. [working: proposed lifecycle]

**Why now:** The primary-purpose document ranks corrupt records and dead instruments above ordinary failures, calls for versions rather than silent edits, and records a cold reader catching its own author's omission. [checked: read origins/persistent_testbed.md §§3, 5–6] The map records a procedure library stalled at stage 1, a generator that would fail on three later additions, and parallel autosurvey/measurement regimes; installing a universal lifecycle by fiat would falsely describe practice. [working: map E04 §§1, 5–6; relationships/themes.md T10] The useful pre-launch requirement is one governed *route* for the launch lane, not instant conformity of all legacy work. [working]

**Minimum artifact / pass condition:** Rehearse this route on one genuine, reversible change, then have a reader other than the author inspect the evidence and the recorded non-actions. A failed but legible rehearsal may meet NIMOI's bar; an untraceable success does not. [working]

### P0, conditional gate — Trust, privacy, and public export

If launch agents can ingest fetched text, act on personal material, contact outsiders, or write to public destinations, settle the relevant boundary **before those powers are granted**: external text remains data; readers of untrusted text lack unrelated write authority; public export requires explicit source/provenance and human release decision; personal/third-party material has a retention and publication decision. If launch is strictly private with those powers withheld, the full handling standard can be developed during bootstrapping, but the withheld powers and escalation route must be explicit. [working: proposed conditional gate]
